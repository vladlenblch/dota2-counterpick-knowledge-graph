## cq03: При каких условиях работает Shackleshot?

This spell can shackle two enemies together, stunning them both. Can shackle the target to an enemy unit or tree at most %shackle_distance% behind the target. Shackleshot can be disjointed. Shackleshot applies to all units and trees behind the target unit and grants Windranger bonus attack damage for each additional shackled unit and tree. Creeps and trees grant less bonus damage.
